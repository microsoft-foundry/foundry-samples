# Contoso SmartThermostat — Product FAQ

## Returns

Opened items can be returned within **30 days** of delivery for a full refund. Unopened items can be returned within **90 days**.

## Warranty

The SmartThermostat includes a **2-year limited warranty** covering manufacturing defects. The warranty does not cover water damage or unauthorized modifications.

## Compatibility

The SmartThermostat works with most **24V HVAC systems**. It is **not** compatible with high-voltage (120V/240V) systems such as electric baseboard heaters.

## Power

The device is powered by the HVAC system's **C-wire**. If your system lacks a C-wire, use the included **Power Adapter Kit**.

## Support hours

Customer support is available **Monday–Friday, 8am–8pm Eastern Time**.
